	**b. VAT.**

### Say It!
1. Can you recommend a good scanner?  
    → **b. These scanners are generally the best.**​
2. What quantity do you need?  
    → **b. I’ll take one white one and three blue ones.**​
3. What’s the price per unit?  
    → **a. They are £50 each before the discount.**​
4. We’re running low on USB chargers.  
    → **a. I’ll order some more.**​
5. I’d like to place an order, please.  
    → **b. Certainly. What do you need exactly?**​
6. What’s the best model?  
    → **b. We’ve got three different models.**​
