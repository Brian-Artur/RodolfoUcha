# Ejercicios Workbook
## Tema 4
### Ejercicio 1
1. Price
2. Payments
3. Payment date
4. Invoice

### Ejercicio 2
1. Pay cash and **we will give you a discount**.
2. We couldn’t order those headsets **because they’re out of stock**.
3. I only need one, **not a large quantity**.
4. They haven’t got any speakers, **so the delivery date won’t be next week**.
5. We only sell **the best quality peripherals**.
6. A box of 10 costs £24, **so the price per unit is £2.40**.
7. The price the sales assistant gave you **doesn’t include VAT**.

### Ejercicio 3
1. Can l charge it?  
	**a. Yes. Can you please tell me the number of your account?**
2. What comes after the subtotal in an invoice?  
	**b. VAT.**
3. Can I pay by cheque?  
	→ **b. I’m sorry, we only accept cash or credit cards.**
4. What goods are we expecting this week?  
    → **a. Keyboards and monitors.**​
5. Does the price include postage and handling?  
    → **b. It will cost more if we’re shipping the goods outside the US.**​

### Ejercicio 4 (Circle TWO correct answers)
1. A maths problem can be …  
    → **a. complicated** y **b. simple**.​
2. This new laptop is …  
    → **b. portable** y **c. practical**.​
3. We’ve got … TV.  
    → **a. a wide‑screen** y **c. a high‑resolution**.​
4. That company only sells … products.  
    → **b. top‑of‑the‑range** y **c. dependable**.​
5. Is this printer … ?  
    → **a. superfast** y **c. reliable**.​
6. Our new computer chairs are …  
    → **a. ergonomic** y **b. sturdy**.​
7. This system is … . That’s its big advantage.  
    → **b. user‑friendly** y **c. flexible**.​
8. Our … PC cases fit in small spaces.  
    → **a. slimline** y **b. compact**.

### Ejercicio 5 (Circle the correct answer)
1. We need to make our network more **secure** for **better protection**.​
2. It’s **compact**. You need **very little** space for it.​
3. You **can’t** remove the parts. They’re **built‑in**.​
4. This is an **all‑in‑one** printer with **a scanner, copier and fax**.​
5. One of the components which makes a computer **powerful** is its **processor**.

### Say It!
1. Can you recommend a good scanner?  
    → **b. These scanners are generally the best.**​
2. What quantity do you need?  
    → **b. I’ll take one white one and three blue ones.**​
3. What’s the price per unit?  
    → **a. They are £50 each before the discount.**​
4. We’re running low on USB chargers.  
    → **a. I’ll order some more.**​
5. I’d like to place an order, please.  
    → **b. Certainly. What do you need exactly?**​
6. What’s the best model?  
    → **b. We’ve got three different models.**​
